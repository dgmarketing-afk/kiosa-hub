Saying it in the team

Read it in groups: “T-H, N, C-L-S-K, zero-two-seven”. A K at position 6 (end of the third group) means no details or photos go to outside agents. If a property's terms change, the letters in the code change with them but the running number stays, so searching by the number alone always finds it.
